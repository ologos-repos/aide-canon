## Position in the canon

AIDK is the **cognitive-theory basis** HCAE rests on:

> **AIDK** (AI has structural epistemic limits) → **HCAE** (so AI work must be human-curated) → **AIDEX** (architectural expression) → **AEON** (control plane)

The architectural claim — that AI must be human-curated — depends on the empirical/epistemic claim that AI has structural limitations only humans can detect and correct.
