# HCAE — Human-Curated, AI-Enabled

A framework for reliable AI deployment grounded in human curation as the locus of judgment and accountability. HCAE is the **practice discipline** that AIDEX expresses architecturally at the experience layer.

## Position in the canon

HCAE sits at the **foundation** tier, **upstream** of AIDEX in the argument lineage:

> **AIDK** (AI has structural epistemic limits) → **HCAE** (so AI work must be human-curated) → **AIDEX** (architectural expression of HCAE at the experience layer) → **AEON** (control plane the deployment lives in)

Subordinating HCAE under `enterprise-platforms/aidex/` would invert the dependency. HCAE pre-exists AIDEX and has its own published Zenodo DOI as canonical citation target.
