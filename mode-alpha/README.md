# mode-alpha/ — Tier 1 (synthesis)

The corpus's **primary orientation mode** — the synthesizing argument that ties Vision-Strategy (Tier 0) to the methodological and instantiation tiers below.

## What this tier holds

A forthcoming synthesizing work titled **AI-enabled Digital Ecosystem**: the integrative architectural argument that explains *how the parts of the canon cohere* — how Foundation (HCAE / AIDK / RLEG) grounds Constructs (DEA / OrdSA / MxM / OAgents), how Constructs compose into Enterprise Platforms (AEON / AIDEX / OAAD), and how the whole stack realizes the Vision-Strategy frame above it.

The synthesis is **not** an academic-degree submission. The label *Mode Alpha* replaces the earlier *Master Thesis* placeholder per [ADR-EA-0007](../decisions/ADR-EA-0007-introduce-vsok-tier-0-and-mode-alpha.md) (the synthesizing artifact's identity is the *primary orientation mode*, not a degree product).

## Status

Reserved. The synthesis lands once constituent DOIs mint and the integrative argument is ready. The corpus commits to populating Mode Alpha over time.

## Why "Mode Alpha"

The Greek-letter convention extends naturally — *Mode Beta*, *Mode Gamma* reserved for future orientation modes if the corpus's intellectual surface broadens. *Alpha* is the corpus's first orientation; siblings are not ruled out, just not present.

(See [ADR-EA-0007 §Alternatives considered (option 3)](../decisions/ADR-EA-0007-introduce-vsok-tier-0-and-mode-alpha.md) for why *Mode Alpha* was preferred over *Mode 0*.)
