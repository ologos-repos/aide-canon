# vision/ — Vision

The **Vision** slot of [VSOK](..) within [Vision-Strategy](../..). The long-horizon outcome the corpus advances toward.

## The Vision

> **AI-centric Digital Ecosystems as an exemplar for next-generation Enterprise IT transformation.**

## Unpacking

**AI-centric Digital Ecosystems** — the canon's namesake (AIDE). Enterprise IT composed *around* AI capability rather than retrofitted to absorb it. The four-plane architecture (control, runtime, experience, capability) ordered by [OrdSA](../../../constructs/ordsa/), oriented by [MxM](../../../constructs/mxm/), populated by [OAgents](../../../constructs/oagents/)-conformant agents, grounded in [HCAE](../../../foundation/hcae/)'s human-curated practice discipline. Not a vendor stack; an enterprise-owned, sovereign architecture.

**As an exemplar** — AIDE is not claimed as the *only* path to enterprise AI architecture. It is the *worked example* — the canonical reference that demonstrates a coherent way to compose enterprise IT for the agentic era. Other paths exist; AIDE is the one developed and proven through this corpus.

**For next-generation Enterprise IT transformation** — the unit of work AIDE addresses is not a project, a product, or a deployment. It is the *transformation of enterprise IT itself* — the structural shift from "IT delivers tools" to "IT composes an AI-native operating environment." Every enterprise IT organization faces this shift; AIDE is the exemplar of how to navigate it coherently.

## What success looks like (horizon: 1–3 years — AI-speed)

"Next-generation" at AI-speed compresses to 1–3 years. The signals below are calibrated to that window — not aspirational decade-out outcomes, but reachable progress markers inside the period when the agentic-era enterprise IT transformation is actively unfolding.

- **AIDE is named in industry conversations** about enterprise AI architecture — cited in enterprise-architect blog posts, conference talks (Gartner / Forrester / Enterprise Architecture summits), analyst notes, and CIO-tier discussion forums.
- **At least one enterprise outside Ologos has stood up an AIDE-shaped reference** — publicly described as such, with the four-plane composition recognizable in their published architecture.
- **OrdSA and OAgents have working third-party implementations** — at least one external project cites the schema/spec as the conformance target; reference-implementation forks visible.
- **MxM is referenced as the named pattern** for harness composition in agentic-architecture discussions — distinct from prompt engineering and from agent-framework lock-in.
- **HCAE appears as the named discipline** in at least one operational governance framework (vendor-neutral) or policy document outside Ologos.
- **The canon is the discoverable hub** — `ologos-repos/aide-canon` shows up in enterprise-architecture search results for the relevant queries; the corpus's Zenodo deposits accrue citations from outside the authors' own work.

## What Vision *isn't*

- Not a product roadmap (those live at the platform level under [`enterprise-platforms/`](../../../enterprise-platforms/))
- Not a sales motion (the corpus is independent research; commercial paths sit elsewhere)
- Not a deadline (1–3 year horizon; success signals accumulate progressively, not on a date)
- Not a guarantee that AIDE is the *only* enterprise AI architecture worth building; it is the *exemplar this corpus develops*

## Methodology — how Vision informs VSOK

Per JD's direction (2026-05-22 evening session), VSOK fleshing-out is **SOTA-driven evidence-based** rather than internal strawman:

> Research the current state-of-the-art against the current AIDE architecture — identify what's already in flight, where AIDE is ahead of, or behind, the game, and what strategies emerge from that — leveraging Hermetic and the soon-to-be-deployed AEON as exemplars.

Vision is the long-horizon endpoint; the SOTA-vs-AIDE gap analysis (housed at [`../../analysis/`](../../analysis/)) is the evidence base from which Strategy refinements, Objectives, and Key Results derive. Hermetic and AEON-deployed serve as the corpus's named exemplars throughout — concrete instances that demonstrate AIDE's claims operationally rather than only in prose.
