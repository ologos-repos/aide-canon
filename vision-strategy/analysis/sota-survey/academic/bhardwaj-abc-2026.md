## 1. What it is

**Agent Behavioral Contracts (ABC)** brings Design-by-Contract to autonomous AI-aides. The thesis: traditional software gets correctness guarantees from formal contracts (type systems, API contracts, assertions), but AI-aides "operate on prompts and natural language instructions" with no formal behavioral specification, no verifiable guarantees, and no enforcement — the root cause of a failure class unique to agentic AI (behavioral drift, governance violations, silent degradation).

ABC's core construct is a contract **C = (P, I, G, R)** — **P**reconditions, **I**nvariants, **G**overnance policies, **R**ecovery mechanisms — as first-class, *runtime-enforceable* components. Around that it contributes: **(p, δ, k)-satisfaction**, a probabilistic compliance notion that accounts for LLM non-determinism and recovery (with a Drift Bounds Theorem bounding behavioral drift); **safe-composition conditions** for multi-agent chains with probabilistic degradation bounds; **AgentAssert**, a runtime enforcement library implementing the model; and **AgentContract-Bench**, a 200-scenario benchmark across 7 models / 6 vendors, evaluated over 1,980 sessions, reporting that contracted agents detect 5.2–6.8 soft violations per session that uncontracted baselines miss. Its altitude is a **formal, runtime, within-session behavioral specification + enforcement** layer — narrower and more formally precise than OAgents' full operational envelope, and the closest external work to it.

ABC maps overwhelmingly to the **OAgents construct** (behavioral envelope), with secondary touchpoints on OrdSA (authority) and the foundation tier (HCAE). The terminology note below is decisive because ABC and OAgents use *the same words* ("behavioral", "contract", "governance", "invariant") for closely related but non-identical constructs.

**Terminology note (collision flag).** ABC's **"contract"** ≈ the OAgents **behavioral envelope** but scoped to a single session and stated as a formal tuple; do not equate them silently. ABC's **"Governance policies (G)"** are *contract-internal clauses* — **not** the canon's governance tier and **not** OrdSA authority; this is a genuine vocabulary collision to watch. Throughout, "agent" in ABC is the casual industry sense (an AI system under instruction) → in canon vocabulary that referent is the **AI-aide** ([ADR-EA-0016](../../../../decisions/ADR-EA-0016-adopt-ai-aide-as-canon-vocabulary.md)); it is **not** the OAgents `Agent` (a typed object inside the envelope). ABC's **"Invariants"** partially overlap OAgents pre/post-execution gates but are stated as runtime-checked predicates rather than gate mechanisms.

## 4. Position / relationship + synthesis

**Independent-convergent — same problem, different axis of completeness.** This is the most important external relationship in the academic slice because ABC and OAgents are the two works that most directly formalize *behavioral guarantees for AI-aides*, and they arrived independently. OAgents §10 states the division precisely: *"ABC focuses on formal runtime specification and enforcement within a session. OAgents focuses on operational completeness across sessions. The frameworks could be composed: ABC governing runtime behavioral invariants, OAgents governing the cross-session operational envelope."* The survey read confirms and sharpens that, axis by axis:

- **Where ABC is ahead / sharper** — *formal precision*. ABC has a probabilistic satisfaction calculus ((p, δ, k) + Drift Bounds Theorem), formal composition conditions with degradation bounds, a runtime enforcement library (AgentAssert), and a published cross-vendor benchmark (AgentContract-Bench, 1,980 sessions). OAgents' conformance is a taxonomy + evidence-review with feasibility-demonstration, not yet empirical validation (its §9.3 says so explicitly) and not a formal satisfaction relation. On *mathematically stated, benchmarked, within-session enforcement*, ABC is the more mature artifact.
- **Where OAgents extends beyond ABC** — *operational completeness + authority + standards alignment*. OAgents' envelope is the **full triad**: pre-execution gates **+** post-execution verification **+** operational discipline (cross-session memory, accountability, anti-confabulation), wrapped in three **conformance levels** (Basic / Standard / Autonomous), aligned as a **NIST AI RMF implementation profile** and to SP 800-207 Zero-Trust, with authority supplied externally by **OrdSA** (O0–O6). ABC is, by design, within-session and authority-agnostic — its "Governance (G)" clauses are not an ordinal authority layer.
- **Where they sit at the same altitude** — *composition*. ABC's safe-composition conditions and the canon's [workflow-orchestration](../../../../patterns/workflow-orchestration.md) envelope-refinement law are independently-derived answers to the same question (how do behavioral guarantees survive chaining); convergent, mutually informative.

**Synthesis — they compose, not compete (and OAgents already says so).** The clean integration is exactly the §10 thesis: **ABC as the formal within-session enforcement kernel, OAgents as the cross-session operational envelope + conformance shell, OrdSA as the authority layer.** ABC could supply the formal satisfaction semantics and AgentAssert-style runtime checking that OAgents' envelope currently specifies as enforcement-backed but does not formalize probabilistically; OAgents supplies the cross-session discipline, conformance levels, and RMF alignment ABC does not claim. This mirrors the **canon-spec ↔ benchmarked-artifact** complementarity, distinct from the canon-spec ↔ platform-substrate relationship the LangChain finding documents.

**Grounded-confidence caveat.** ABC's *headline structure* is confirmed across the arXiv abstract, the project site, and the OAgents reference (high confidence: tuple, satisfaction notion, AgentAssert, benchmark). Deeper internals — the precise statement of the Drift Bounds Theorem, exact composition preconditions, and benchmark scoring methodology — were **not** read in full from the paper body at survey time; the per-axis "ahead/behind" calls above on *formal rigor* are inferred from the abstract + secondary summaries and should be re-checked against the full PDF before any citation in canon-published work. No peer-reviewed venue was confirmed (pre-print as of survey).

## 5. Objective implication

Three Doerr-style Objective shapes follow:

1. **Converge-or-differentiate (formal enforcement kernel).** ABC is the SOTA target for *formal, benchmarked, within-session* behavioral enforcement — the exact axis where OAgents is feasibility-demonstrated but not empirically validated (§9.3). KR shape: a documented **"ABC-as-kernel" composition mapping** (ABC contract tuple → OAgents pre/post-execution gates; AgentAssert → OAgents reference enforcement) that makes the §10 "could be composed" claim concrete and citable.
2. **Catch-up (empirical validation).** AgentContract-Bench is a published cross-vendor benchmark; OAgents has conformance *criteria* but no benchmark. KR shape: run an OAgents-conformant exemplar against an AgentContract-Bench-style harness and publish the result as the empirical validation §9.3 invites.
3. **Defend-and-extend (operational + authority lead).** OAgents' cross-session envelope, conformance levels, NIST-RMF profiling, and OrdSA authority are all *absent* from ABC. KR shape: a one-page "what the envelope adds beyond a within-session contract" note — operational discipline + conformance levels + RMF alignment + ordinal authority — usable as the differentiation statement in OAgents' own Related Work and in any joint citation.
