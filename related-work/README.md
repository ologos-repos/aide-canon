# related-work/

Allied research that informs the AIDE canon without being part of its spine. Distinguished from `foundation/` (upstream of the architecture's argument) and `constructs/` (methodological patterns within the architecture) — related work travels adjacent to the canon, with its own publishing identity, but is hosted here for reading proximity.

## Provenance

Each related-work artifact is hosted as-is, with attribution and DOI/source pointer in its subdir README. Modifications to related work are not made within the canon; if the author publishes a revised version, the canon's copy is updated to match.
